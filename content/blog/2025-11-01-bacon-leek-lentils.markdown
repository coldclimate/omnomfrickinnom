As winter starts to set in, and because I had to make use of some bits, this lunch time I knocked up this very savoury lentil and baocn soup.  

## You will need

Everything here is by volume because I didn't weigh anything.   I'm using an American cup because roughly 1 cup of lentils wasa big lunch for two people.  The InstantPot comes into it's own again here with mixed modes.

* 1x dried green lentils
* 1x chopped leek
* 0.25 chopped bacon
* a spooful of creme fresh to top

## Do

* Saute the leek and bacon in a teaspoon of oil until the leek is softened and the bacon starts to get a bit crispy
* Add the lentils and 4x of water
* Pressure cook on high pressure for 10 minutes
* Leave to let the pressure off naturally

## Result

Salty, savoury, the lentils are chewy, a blob of creme fresh balanced it nicely.
